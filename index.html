About Sanjeevam

Rooted in an old idea of healing

“In our tradition, Sanjeevani restored a life that seemed lost. We carry that promise into modern medicine.”

Sanjeevam Health Care was founded with a simple belief: good medicine is equal parts skill and compassion. Our clinicians combine current, evidence based practice with the patience to listen, explain and involve families in every decision.

We are building a hospital where patients feel known by name, where costs are explained upfront, and where the environment itself, full of light, air and greenery, supports recovery.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can I visit the OPD without prior notice?

Yes. Walk ins are welcome during OPD hours, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM.

Which insurance providers do you accept?

We support cashless treatment with most leading insurers and TPAs. Our insurance desk will verify your policy and guide you through approvals.

Can I get my reports online?

Yes. Laboratory and imaging reports are shared on WhatsApp and email as soon as they are ready, and your doctor will explain them at your follow up.

Is the emergency department open at night?

Our emergency department, ICU and pharmacy are open 24 hours a day, every day of the year.
